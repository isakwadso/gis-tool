About this map

Coloured areas are permanent pastures where recent and forecast weather has suited the field mushroom (Agaricus campestris). Pick a day with the slider: the score for a day uses the five days ending on that day.

How the score works

    The score is a relative index, not a probability. Weather is calculated for a grid of roughly 5 km squares, so neighbouring pastures share a score. "Past" days are weather-model data, not station measurements. Rain forecasts more than about five days ahead are uncertain.

    Before you pick

    Field mushrooms can be confused with toxic species, including the yellow-staining mushroom (Agaricus xanthodermus) and deadly white amanitas. Only eat mushrooms you have identified with certainty.

    Pastures are private land. Follow allemansrätten: keep your distance from grazing animals, close gates behind you, and stay out of gardens and fields with crops.
